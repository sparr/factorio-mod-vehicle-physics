Version: 2.1.2
Date: ????
  Features:
    - Boats and aircraft are told apart from cars and handled accordingly. A boat picks up
      and sheds speed a quarter as readily as a car, slides four times as much and turns an
      eighth as fast; an aircraft a tenth as readily, slides ten times as much, and turns a
      quarter as fast
    - Neither boats nor aircraft leave tire marks, and neither throws up dust
  Bugfixes:
    - A drifting boat is no longer pushed onto land, where it could not move at all. The
      same keeps a drifting car out of the water
    - The physics applies to a vehicle you were already sitting in when the mod was added
      to a game. It used to do nothing at all until you got out and back in
    - Getting into a vehicle and straight back out no longer leaves it being tracked with
      nobody in it
